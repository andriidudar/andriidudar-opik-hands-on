# Basil (Ocimum basilicum)
Light: 6 hours of sun, warm nights above 10 C (50 F). Water: keep soil lightly moist, morning watering.
Harvest: pinch just above a leaf pair to make the plant bush out; remove flower buds as soon as they show.
Common problems: downy mildew (yellow leaves with grey fuzz underneath), cold damage (black leaves).
Tip: it sulks on a cold windowsill; keep it away from glass in winter.
