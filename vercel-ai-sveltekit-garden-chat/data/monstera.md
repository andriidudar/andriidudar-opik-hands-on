# Monstera (Monstera deliciosa), indoor
Light: bright indirect light; direct afternoon sun scorches leaves. Water: when the top 5 cm (2 in) of soil is dry, roughly every 7-10 days.
Feed: half-strength liquid feed monthly in spring and summer, none in winter.
Common problems: yellow lower leaves (overwatering), brown crispy edges (dry air or underwatering), no splits in new leaves (too little light).
Tip: give it a moss pole; aerial roots climb and the leaves get bigger.
