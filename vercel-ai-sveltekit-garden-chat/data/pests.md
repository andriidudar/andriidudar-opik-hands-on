# Common garden pests
Aphids: clusters of tiny insects on new growth. Blast with water, then wipe with diluted soapy water; ladybirds help.
Slugs and snails: holes in leaves overnight. Hand-pick at dusk, use copper tape around pots, water in the morning.
Spider mites (indoor and hot dry spots): fine webbing and stippled leaves. Raise humidity, wipe leaves, repeat weekly for a month.
Fungus gnats: tiny flies over houseplant soil. Let the top layer dry out and use yellow sticky traps.
