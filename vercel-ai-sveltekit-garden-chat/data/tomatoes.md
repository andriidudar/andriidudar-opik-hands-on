# Tomatoes (Solanum lycopersicum)
Light: 8+ hours of direct sun. Water: deeply, 2-3 times a week, at the base, never on the leaves.
Feed: balanced fertilizer at planting, then a high-potassium feed once fruit sets.
Common problems: blossom end rot (uneven watering, low calcium), early blight (brown rings on lower leaves; remove them and mulch).
Tip: pinch out side shoots on cordon varieties; leave them on bush varieties.
