# Compost basics
Balance: roughly 2 parts brown (dry leaves, cardboard) to 1 part green (kitchen scraps, grass clippings).
Moisture: like a wrung-out sponge. Turn every 1-2 weeks to add air.
Too wet and smelly: add browns and turn. Too dry and slow: add greens and water.
Never add: meat, dairy, cooked oils, or diseased plants such as tomatoes with blight.
Ready in 2-6 months when dark, crumbly and smelling of forest soil.
