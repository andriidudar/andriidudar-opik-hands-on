# Succulents (echeveria, jade, haworthia)
Light: bright light; echeveria wants some direct sun, haworthia prefers bright shade. Water: soak, then let the soil dry completely, about every 2-3 weeks, less in winter.
Soil: gritty cactus mix in a pot with a drainage hole.
Common problems: mushy translucent leaves (overwatering), stretched pale growth (etiolation, not enough light).
Tip: a leaf that falls off can be laid on dry soil and will root in a few weeks.
