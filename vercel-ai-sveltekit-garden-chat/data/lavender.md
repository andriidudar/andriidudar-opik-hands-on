# Lavender (Lavandula angustifolia)
Light: full sun. Soil: poor, gritty and very well drained; hates wet winters. Water: only in the first season, then rarely.
Pruning: trim by a third right after flowering in late summer; never cut into old bare wood.
Common problems: root rot in heavy soil, woody centres on plants that were never pruned.
Tip: skip fertilizer and rich compost, which give leaves and few flowers.
