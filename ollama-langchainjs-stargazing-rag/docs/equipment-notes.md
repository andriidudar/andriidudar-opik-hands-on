# Fernhill Astronomical Society: Equipment Notes

## Club loaner telescopes
The club owns three loaners. The 8-inch Dobsonian "Barnaby" is the easiest to use and is the one handed to new members. The 10-inch Dobsonian "Orla" needs about 40 minutes to cool to ambient temperature before it gives sharp views. The 80 mm refractor "Pip" is on an equatorial mount and is the loaner for outreach nights. Loaners are booked through the equipment secretary, Marguerite Osei, for one week at a time.

## Eyepieces and filters
The shared eyepiece case holds 25 mm, 15 mm, 10 mm and 6 mm Plossl eyepieces plus a 2x Barlow. The UHC nebula filter lives in the red pouch and helps most on the Orion Nebula and the Veil. The O-III filter is the better choice for the Veil and the Ring Nebula but needs the 10-inch aperture. Never touch eyepiece glass with fingers; use the blower first, then the lens tissue.

## Dew control
Dew heater strips and the 12 V battery pack are stored with the Pip refractor. Run the heater at half power; full power causes tube currents that blur the image. Dew shields cost almost nothing and are the first thing to fit on a refractor.

## Collimation
Barnaby and Orla are collimated with a Cheshire eyepiece and a laser collimator. Check collimation at the start of every session, not just after transport. The Cheshire is kept in the green pouch.
