# Fernhill Astronomical Society: Target Cheat Sheet

## Spring targets
Best galaxies: the Leo Triplet, M81 and M82 in Ursa Major, the Whirlpool Galaxy (M51) in Canes Venatici. The Virgo Cluster is dense enough that a dark site shows a dozen galaxies in one sweep of a 10-inch Dobsonian.

## Summer targets
The Ring Nebula (M57) and the Dumbbell Nebula (M27) are easy in the 8-inch. M13 and M92 in Hercules are the classic globular clusters. The Milky Way core rises low in the south from Quarry Ridge, so use a wide-field eyepiece.

## Planets
Check the Sky Calendar page on the club website for the month's visibility windows. Saturn's ring tilt in 2026 is shallow, so detail on the ring system is limited; Titan is the easiest moon to find. Jupiter is a morning object from August.

## Double stars for poor seeing
When seeing is below 3/5 try Albireo, Mizar and Alcor, or Gamma Andromedae. They keep their charm at modest magnification where planetary detail fails.
