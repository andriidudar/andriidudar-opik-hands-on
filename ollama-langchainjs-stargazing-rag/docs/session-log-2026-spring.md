# Fernhill Astronomical Society: Observing Log, Spring 2026

## 14 March 2026: Messier Marathon night, Cobb Field
Eleven members attended. Seeing was 4/5 and transparency was excellent, with a limiting magnitude of 6.4 at the zenith. The Moon was a thin crescent and set at 20:10. We logged 87 Messier objects before cloud rolled in from the west at 03:40. Highlights were M81 and M82 in the same field of view of the 10-inch Dobsonian, and a crisp split of the Double Cluster in Perseus. Dew became a problem after midnight; Priya's heater strips kept her 8-inch refractor clear while two other scopes fogged over.

## 11 April 2026: Galaxy season at Quarry Ridge
Six members, temperature 3 C, light breeze. Seeing was 3/5 and transparency was good. The Leo Triplet (M65, M66, NGC 3628) fit in one eyepiece view at 60x. Tomasz sketched the Whirlpool Galaxy (M51) and noted the faint bridge to NGC 5195. The Sky Quality Meter read 21.3 mag/arcsec2 at 23:00, the best reading of the season. A satellite flare from a Starlink train crossed Ursa Major at 21:48.

## 9 May 2026: Globular clusters and the first Saturn of the year
Nine members at Cobb Field. Saturn rose at 01:15 and was observed from 01:40; the rings were tilted only about 3 degrees, nearly edge-on, and Titan was visible beside the planet. M13 resolved to the core in the 10-inch Dobsonian at 150x. M92 was a pleasant surprise nearby. Haze reduced transparency to 2/5 after 02:00 and we packed up at 02:30.
