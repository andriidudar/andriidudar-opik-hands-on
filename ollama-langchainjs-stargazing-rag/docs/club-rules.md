# Fernhill Astronomical Society: Observing Night Rules

## Lights and cars
Only red torches are allowed on the field. Phone screens must be on the dimmest red setting. Park facing outward and do not use headlights after 21:00. Arrivals after 22:00 must walk in from the gate.

## Supervision and safety
At least two members must be on site at all times. Nobody leaves alone after 01:00. The session leader carries the first-aid kit and the thermos of hot water.

## Logging
Every session leader fills in the observing log within 48 hours: date, site, attendance, seeing, transparency, and notable objects seen. Sketches are scanned and added to the shared folder. The log is published for members on the first Monday of the following month.

## Visitors
Guests are welcome at the Rooftop at the Library public nights, on the second Friday of each month. Visitors under 14 must be accompanied by an adult.
