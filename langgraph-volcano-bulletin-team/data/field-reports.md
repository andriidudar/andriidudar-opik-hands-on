# Mount Tarrow Observatory — Field reports and ground deformation, week of 12-18 October

## Ground deformation

- The GNSS station at the summit rim moved 14 mm upward and 6 mm outward over the week, against about 1 mm a week in the past year.
- Tiltmeter TLT2 on the north flank recorded 9 microradians of inflation, mostly on Wednesday and Thursday.
- InSAR passes on Friday showed a roughly 3 cm uplift bulge centred 800 m south of the crater lake. Uplift is not seen on the east or west flanks.

## Field team notes

- Rangers closed the Ridgeline trail above the hut on Thursday after a rockfall from the south wall. Two small ash-free steam plumes, about 150 m high, were seen from the Cinder Pass overlook on Saturday.
- A hiker reported a sulfur smell at the Ridgeline hut on Thursday at about 04:00. No symptoms were reported.
- Water sampling at the Ashgrove stream on Friday showed a pH of 5.9, down from 6.8 in September.
- Ashgrove valley residents were told to expect more felt shaking and no ashfall; the observatory held a community call on Friday with 62 attendees.

## Instrument status

- Station TRW3 was offline from Tuesday evening to Thursday noon with a failed solar controller. Events on the east flank were located using three stations rather than four, which widens depth uncertainty to about 0.6 km.
- The multi-gas box was recalibrated on Monday.
