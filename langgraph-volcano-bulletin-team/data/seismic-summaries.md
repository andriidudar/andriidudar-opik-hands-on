# Mount Tarrow Observatory — Seismograph summaries, week of 12-18 October

Station network: TRW1 (summit rim), TRW2 (north flank), TRW3 (east flank), TRW4 (Ashgrove valley).

## Monday and Tuesday

- Monday: 41 volcano-tectonic (VT) earthquakes, largest M1.6 at 2.1 km depth beneath the summit crater. Background is about 12 per day.
- Tuesday: 58 VT earthquakes, largest M1.9. Hypocenters shallowed to 1.4 km depth under the crater's south wall.
- Tremor amplitude at TRW1 held near 0.8 micrometres per second, unchanged from September.

## Wednesday and Thursday

- Wednesday: 73 VT earthquakes, the first daily total above 70 since March. Largest event M2.2 at 1.1 km depth.
- Thursday: 96 VT earthquakes in a swarm lasting 5 hours, 02:10 to 07:15 local time. Largest M2.4, felt by two hikers at the Ridgeline hut.
- Harmonic tremor appeared at TRW1 and TRW2 for 40 minutes on Thursday afternoon, peaking at 2.3 micrometres per second.

## Friday to Sunday

- Friday: 64 VT earthquakes; the swarm decayed but did not return to background.
- Saturday: 49 events, largest M1.5. Tremor absent.
- Sunday: 44 events. The weekly total was 425, against a four-week average of 118.
- Long-period (LP) events stayed rare: 3 in the whole week, all on Thursday, all beneath 1 km.
