# Mount Tarrow Observatory — Gas and thermal readings, week of 12-18 October

Instruments: a ground-based DOAS scanner at the Cinder Pass overlook for sulfur dioxide, a multi-gas box on the crater rim, and a thermal camera on the east ridge.

## Sulfur dioxide flux

- Monday 280 tonnes per day, Wednesday 410, Thursday 920 (the highest reading this year), Saturday 540, Sunday 470.
- The long-run baseline is 150 to 250 tonnes per day.
- Thursday's scan was made in clear air at wind speed 6 m/s; the observatory rates it high confidence.

## Crater rim multi-gas

- The carbon dioxide to sulfur dioxide ratio rose from 3.1 on Monday to 6.8 on Friday, a sign that gas is coming from deeper magma.
- Hydrogen sulfide was detected on the rim on Thursday for the first time since 2022.

## Thermal camera and ground temperature

- The crater lake temperature at the east ridge camera rose from 31 C to 38 C over the week.
- A new fumarole field about 40 m across appeared on the south wall and reached 214 C on Friday.
- Ground temperature at the old fumaroles near the Ashgrove trailhead did not change.
