# Mount Tarrow Observatory — Alert level guidance

## Current level

Mount Tarrow is at alert level YELLOW (advisory) and has been since 3 April. The aviation colour code is GREEN.

## Criteria for raising to ORANGE

- Sustained daily VT counts above 150 for three days, or shallow swarms with hypocenters above 1 km depth.
- Sulfur dioxide flux above 1,000 tonnes per day on two consecutive scans.
- Deformation rates above 25 mm a week at the summit GNSS station.
- Any confirmed magmatic gas signature together with accelerating seismicity.

## Communication schedule

- At YELLOW the observatory publishes a weekly bulletin every Monday at 09:00.
- A special statement is issued within 6 hours of any criterion being met, and the county emergency office is called first.
- The next scheduled review of the alert level is 22 October.
