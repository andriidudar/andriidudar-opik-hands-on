# Quillfall (2-6 players, card drafting)

## Setup
Shuffle the 90 feather cards and deal 7 to each player. Place the remaining cards as the roost deck and flip the top card to start the discard row. Feathers come in four colors (ember, frost, moss, dusk) and are numbered 1 to 9.

## Playing a round
On your turn, play one feather onto the discard row. It must match the color or be within 2 numbers of the top feather. If you cannot, draw from the roost deck until you can, up to 3 cards; if you still cannot play, you pass and keep your hand.

## Quills
Playing a feather numbered 9 lets you take a quill token from the supply. A quill can be spent to ignore the color-or-number rule once, for any one play. You may hold at most 2 quills; extra quills are returned to the supply.

## Molting
When the roost deck runs out, shuffle all the discard row except the top card to make a new roost deck. This is called a molt. After the second molt, hand size limits are lifted: players no longer draw to refill.

## Ending the game
The round ends as soon as a player empties their hand. Other players score 1 point per feather left in hand, doubled for dusk feathers. First player to reach 15 points loses, and the player with the fewest points at that moment wins.
