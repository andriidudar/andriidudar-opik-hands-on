# Tidewrack (2-4 players, salvage and trading)

## Setup
Lay the 16 reef tiles in a 4x4 grid, face down. Each player takes a skiff token, 3 rope tokens and 5 coins. Shuffle the wreck deck and deal 2 wreck cards to each player; keep them hidden. The youngest player is the first Harbormaster.

## Turn structure
On your turn you get 3 actions, chosen from: Sail (move your skiff to an adjacent tile), Dive (flip the tile you are on and take its salvage), Trade (swap one salvage with another player on the same tile) or Mend (spend 1 rope to remove a leak token). You may repeat actions. After your actions, the Harbormaster rotates clockwise.

## Diving and salvage
When you Dive, flip the tile and draw salvage tokens equal to the number printed on it. If the tile shows a whirlpool, you lose 1 rope instead and your skiff returns to the harbor. A tile can only be dived once; leave it face up afterwards.

## Leaks
A skiff that holds more than 4 salvage tokens gains a leak token at the end of the turn. A skiff with 3 leak tokens sinks: its owner discards all salvage and restarts from the harbor on their next turn, but keeps coins and wreck cards.

## Winning
The game ends when the last reef tile is flipped. Each salvage token delivered to the harbor scores 2 coins, and each completed wreck card scores 5 coins. The player with the most coins wins; ties go to the player with fewer leak tokens.
