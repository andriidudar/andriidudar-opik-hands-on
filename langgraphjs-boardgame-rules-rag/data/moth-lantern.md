# Moth Lantern (3-5 players, hidden movement)

## Setup
One player is the Lantern Keeper; everyone else plays a Moth. The Keeper places the lantern miniature on the center cavern tile and receives 6 shade cards. Each Moth starts on a different edge tile with 2 dust tokens and secretly writes down a destination tile, which must be at least 4 tiles away.

## Moth turn
On your turn, move up to 3 tiles through connected caverns. You may spend 1 dust token to move 1 extra tile, but never more than 4 tiles in a turn. A Moth cannot end its movement on a tile containing another Moth.

## The Keeper's beam
At the end of each round the Keeper points the lantern along a straight line of up to 5 tiles. Any Moth on a lit tile must reveal itself and loses 1 dust token. A Moth with no dust to lose is scorched and is out of the game.

## Shade cards
The Keeper may play one shade card per round to block a tile from the beam. Moths may not play shade cards. Each shade card can be used once, then it is discarded. When the Keeper has no shade cards left, the beam range grows to 7 tiles.

## Winning
A Moth wins by revealing its destination tile and standing on it at the start of its turn. If every Moth is scorched, or the Keeper completes 10 rounds without any Moth reaching its destination, the Keeper wins.
