# Loafwright BM-4 Bread Maker: Owner's Manual

## 1. Loaf sizes

The BM-4 bakes three loaf sizes: 500 g, 750 g and 1000 g. The size refers to the weight of the baked loaf. The pan must never contain more than 600 g of flour in total, or the dough may rise into the lid and burn on the heating element.

## 2. Programs

Program 1 (Basic White) takes 3 hours 10 minutes. Program 2 (Wholemeal) takes 3 hours 50 minutes. Program 3 (Gluten-Free) takes 2 hours 45 minutes and has no second rise. Program 4 (Dough) kneads and proves the dough for 1 hour 30 minutes but does not bake it. Program 5 (Jam) runs for 1 hour 20 minutes.

## 3. Delay timer

You can delay the start by up to 13 hours. Do not use the delay timer with recipes that contain fresh milk, eggs, cheese or fresh fruit, because these ingredients can spoil before baking begins. Always keep the yeast apart from the liquid when using the delay timer: place the flour first, the liquid second, and the yeast on top of the flour in a small dip.

## 4. The kneading paddle

The kneading paddle is removable. If the paddle stays in the loaf after baking, wait ten minutes for the loaf to cool, then lift the loaf out and remove the paddle with the hook tool. Never use a metal knife, as this scratches the non-stick coating of the pan.

## 5. Troubleshooting

If the display shows "E01" the pan temperature is too low: the room is below 10 °C or the pan is not seated correctly. If the display shows "E02" the pan temperature is too high; unplug the machine and let it cool for 60 minutes. If the loaf collapses in the middle, you most likely used too much yeast or too much liquid.

## 6. Power failure

If power is interrupted for less than 10 minutes during operation, the BM-4 resumes the program where it stopped. After a longer interruption the program does not resume, and the dough must be discarded if it has not yet started baking.

## 7. Warranty

The BM-4 is covered by a 1 year limited warranty. The non-stick coating of the pan is considered a wearing part and is covered for 90 days only.
