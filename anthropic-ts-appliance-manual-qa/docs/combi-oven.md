# Hearthline CO-900 Combi Oven: Owner's Manual

## 1. Cooking modes

The CO-900 offers three cooking modes. Dry Heat uses the fan and the heating elements only and is suited to roasting and baking. Steam Mode fills the cavity with steam at 100 °C and is suited to vegetables, fish and reheating. Combi Mode blends both: you choose a steam level from 10% to 90% and a temperature from 120 °C to 230 °C.

The maximum temperature in Dry Heat is 250 °C. Steam Mode cannot be set above 100 °C.

## 2. Water supply

The CO-900 draws water from the 1.8 litre removable tank on the left side of the oven. Use only filtered or softened water. Hard water causes limescale, and limescale damage is not covered by the warranty.

When the tank holds less than 300 ml, the display shows the message "Fill tank" and the oven pauses any steam program until the tank is refilled and the door is closed.

## 3. Descaling

The oven counts steam hours. After 60 hours of steam operation the display shows "Descale now". You may continue to cook in Dry Heat mode, but Steam Mode and Combi Mode are locked until a descaling cycle has been completed. Run the descaling cycle with one Hearthline DescaleKit sachet dissolved in a full tank. The cycle takes 75 minutes and must not be interrupted.

## 4. Cleaning

Select Auto-Clean from the menu to run a 40 minute rinse cycle. Never use oven spray on the cavity; it damages the steam sensor. Remove the door gasket only when the cavity has cooled below 50 °C. Wipe the gasket with a damp cloth and mild soap.

## 5. Safety

Do not open the door during a steam program unless the display shows "Safe to open". Escaping steam can cause burns. Keep children away from the door while the oven is operating, because the glass can reach 70 °C on the outside.

## 6. Warranty

The CO-900 carries a 2 year limited warranty on parts and labour. The steam generator is covered for 5 years. The warranty does not cover damage caused by limescale, by the use of oven spray in the cavity, or by operation without the supplied drip tray.
