# Brasa Ristretto E2 Espresso Machine: Owner's Manual

## 1. Warm-up

Switch the machine on with the main power switch at the back. The boiler needs 8 minutes to reach brewing temperature, and the group head needs 20 minutes to be fully heat-soaked. For the best espresso, wait the full 20 minutes before pulling your first shot.

## 2. Brewing pressure

The E2 has a pump pressure gauge on the front panel. The ideal brewing pressure is 9 bar. If the gauge shows more than 11 bar during extraction, the coffee is ground too fine; if it stays below 7 bar, the grind is too coarse or the dose is too small.

## 3. Dose and basket sizes

The single basket takes 9 g to 10 g of ground coffee. The double basket takes 17 g to 19 g. Never use the double basket with a single dose, as the water finds a channel through the puck.

## 4. Steam wand

Purge the steam wand for two seconds before and after every use. The steam wand is ready as soon as the steam light turns green, about 45 seconds after the brew light. Never leave the wand tip submerged in milk when you close the steam valve, as milk can be sucked back into the boiler.

## 5. Backflushing

Backflush the group head with plain water at the end of every day. Once a week, backflush with one level teaspoon of Brasa BlackClean powder: run the pump for 10 seconds, pause for 10 seconds, and repeat five times, then rinse with five cycles of plain water.

## 6. Water tank and filter

The water tank holds 2.4 litres. The machine has a water level sensor. When the tank is nearly empty, the pump is disabled and the red "Refill" light flashes. Replace the tank filter cartridge every 3 months or after 120 litres of water, whichever comes first.

## 7. Warranty

The E2 comes with a 2 year warranty. The warranty is void if the machine is operated without water in the tank, or if the boiler is descaled with any product other than Brasa DescaleLiquid.
