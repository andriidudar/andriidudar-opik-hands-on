L01 The Republic of Tamarind Cay is an archipelago of 14 inhabited islands; its capital is Port Selwyn on the island of Greater Cay.
L02 The national population was 212,000 at the 2023 census, and 61% of residents live in Port Selwyn.
L03 The official currency is the cay shilling, issued by the Bank of Tamarind since 1971.
L04 The national language is Cayan; English is used in courts and in schools above grade 6.
L05 The Lantern Festival is held on the first full moon of November and lasts three nights.
L06 The highest point is Mount Orrin at 412 metres, on the island of Little Orrin.
L07 The Selwyn Causeway, opened in 1988, links Greater Cay to the island of Pell and is 3.4 kilometres long.
L08 Ferries between the islands are free for residents and cost 8 shillings for visitors.
L09 The main export is dried tamarind pulp, followed by sea salt and woven palm goods.
L10 Voting age is 17, and the 31-seat Assembly is elected every four years.
L11 The Tamarind Cay Observatory on Mount Orrin was founded in 1994 and is staffed by nine researchers.
L12 Rainfall peaks between June and September; the dry season runs from January to March.
L13 The national bird is the crimson tern, which nests only on the island of Pell.
L14 Port Selwyn's harbour handles about 40 cargo ships per month.
